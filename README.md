A Unity card game at the intersection of quantum computing and Uno. The
familiar rules are still there; superposition, entanglement and quantum gates
are the cards on top of them, so the physics is something you play rather than
something you read.

There is a browser rewrite with online multiplayer at
[Qno-web](https://github.com/MayurJivani/Qno-web), playable at
[qno.futile.studio](https://qno.futile.studio).

Open the project folder in Unity and press play.
